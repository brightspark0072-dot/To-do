How streaks and scores work
  • A day is a success when you finish at least 70% of its habits. Change this in More.
  • Today is "in progress" until you reach the goal. It never breaks your streak.
  • A missed or not-tracked past day ends the current streak. Your best streak is always kept.
  • A rest day pauses everything: it neither breaks nor adds to a streak and is left out of the scores.
  • Consistency = successful days ÷ (successful + missed + not-tracked) over the last 30 finished days since you started.
  • The 7-day average uses the last 7 finished days. Days you forgot to track count as 0%, so the number stays honest.
  • One bad day is just one day. Look at consistency, not only the streak.

Your data and privacy

Everything stays on this device. VYRON has no account, no server and no tracking. Data lives in your browser's local storage, which is not encrypted and can be erased if you clear site data, uninstall the browser or switch phones. Export a backup regularly and keep it somewhere private.
